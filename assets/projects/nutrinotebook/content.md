## 

### Background

The NSW government hopes to help young people to build a healthy lifestyle, through enhancing their nutrition knowledge and establishing healthy eating habits.



###### Diary Study

We've done the Diary Study to understand specific problems users might meet, and turn it into tables



By categorized and re-ordered data, we found the most important problems to our target user.



### 

### Persona

We’ve gained the surveys from target user, collected the effective data to conclude our persona.



Despite her organized nature, she struggles to establish a consistent healthy-eating routine because she lacks practical methods, finds traditional food-tracking too time-consuming, and quickly loses motivation when results are not immediately visible. Her primary goal is to integrate reliable nutrition knowledge into a simple, sustainable system that fits her busy work schedule and supports long-term wellness.



### Strategy

1 - Help user quickly take record

* AI assistant
* Voice Input
* Uploading Image

2 - Help user increase nutrition knowledge

* Recommend Articles
* Set Calendar Remind

3 - Help user build recording habit

* Setting goals
* Social connection



### Strategy - 1

###### Flexible Control

User can manually stop generation, and click to continue

###### Accuracy

If input is ambiguous, AI assist will ask for more information to confirm, so that has improved the accuracy of generation result

###### Image Pick-up Easy Selecting

Using images to clear illustrate the food type, helping user quickly select and record their intake

###### Categorize Nutrition

When recording, automatically categorize food in different types of nutrition. Help to accurately record.

###### Card Sorting

Using card-style design, displays nutritional intake in categories, and makes data clear at a glance. **Data visualization makes it easier for users to read information efficiently**

###### Added background prompt

Using background colour and shadow to highlight the latest recording

**Differentiated display to indicate information categories**



### Strategy - 2

###### calendar

Provide monthly and weekly recordings to help users check and review their status easily. Visualise the amount to show intake serves

###### Accurate Reviewing

Weekly Records show details in specific time and emphasize the amount of serves. Users can pick up valid information immediately



**Provide different recommendations**

**according to users' preference**



### Strategy - 3

###### Display Goals Achievement

Visualizing targets to remind users their process, which can motivate user to achieve their goals

###### Share with friends

Through the friend sharing mechanism, users are encouraged to participate in nutrition knowledge learning, record, and establish good nutrition habits



Provides various emoji to allow users express their emotions, creating a good vibe for users



### 

### Theme Color

###### Theme

Choose deep green to represent health theme

###### Normal

Increase grayscale and decrease saturation to reduce the eye strain caused by colors, thereby establishing a reliable and professional tool image.

###### Minor

Increase brightness and color vibrancy to match the distinct personalities of young users.
